## Wall-clock ETA — correction to a false number produced during this update

An intermediate script of mine printed **83.3 min per 5k**, implying 27.8 h
remaining. That was a bug in my own script, not a real slowdown: it subtracted step
*numbers* instead of timestamps. Resolved against two independent sources:

- checkpoint mtime deltas, 34 consecutive 5k blocks: all **61.6-61.9 min**
- process `etimes` 129,903 s over 175,016 steps: **0.7422 s/step = 61.9 min/5k**

Both give **~21.2 h remaining**. The `eta:` field in the training log still
understates by **40.3%** (12.7 h against 21.2 h) because `total_training_time`
accumulates only forward/backward `dt` and excludes eval, saves and wandb
(Finding 12). Wall-clock figures are quoted throughout.
