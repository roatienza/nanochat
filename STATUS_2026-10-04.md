## Why v2 beat v1

a 2.019 B-parameter model at `param_data_ratio` 8 — 21.1 passes over the same data.
Its val turned upward at ~step 40,000 and had climbed to 0.939570 by the step it
died. v2 pulled 170 shards (9.150 B tokens, 1.03 passes), became a ~1-epoch run,
and improved to the final step.

I misdiagnosed this as an LR-schedule issue before the Sep 30 restart, which cost
~12 h of redone work. Finding 8 (no warmdown turnaround) had been re-derived in
the log but never reconciled against Finding 12 (dataset sizing).
